FAQ

Frequently asked questions

  • The grading looks wrong.

    AI grading can make mistakes. In the explanation view you can point at the part that looks wrong and ask for another look. Still not convinced? Send us an email.

  • I want to cancel my subscription.

    Google Play app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Billing and refunds are handled by Google Play, so refund requests are also fastest through Google Play.

  • Will I lose my learning history if I switch devices?

    Learning data is stored on your device and is not synced with the server, so it does not carry over when you switch devices.

  • What happens to the sentences I write?

    We store and process them only to the extent needed for grading and review. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

  • I want to delete my account and data.

    You can delete them in the app's Settings. If you cannot access the app, email us at the address above.
