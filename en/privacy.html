GenRecall

Effective date: 2026-06-22 · Last revised: 2026-10-01

This English translation is provided for convenience only. The Korean original is the legally binding version and prevails in case of any discrepancy.

GenRecall (the "Service") takes your personal data seriously and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what data the Service collects and how it is used, stored, and destroyed.

Summary: Your learning history (the answers you write, scores, AI explanations, review schedule, statistics, favorites, notes, your “Ask the teacher” conversations, and level test records) is stored on your device and on the server, per account — so that you can pick up where you left off by signing in with the same account, even after switching devices or reinstalling the app. Beyond this, the server stores information needed to run the Service, such as your account, subscription, and usage. To grade your writing with AI, the sentences you write are sent to the grading providers (OpenAI, Anthropic). Free users are shown advertising, and an advertising identifier is sent to Google when ads are served. Usage statistics are sent to Google only if you choose “Turn on” in the app, and never include the sentences you write or your account details (see 8-1 below). Some data is processed outside the Republic of Korea, including in the United States, to provide the Service (see 3-1 below).

Account and data deletion requests

If you would like your GenRecall account and related data deleted, you can request it in either of these ways.

  • In the app: Use "Delete account" in the Settings screen to delete your server account and associated data (including the learning history stored on the server), together with the learning history stored on your device.
  • By email: Write to support@existential-labs.com with "Account deletion request" in the subject line, including the email address you signed up with.

Within 7 business days of receiving a request, we delete the following data stored on the server: account information (email, name, sign-in account details), subscription status, learning history, level-test records and reviews, pending grading items, and feedback you sent. Learning records stored on your device are removed via "Delete account" or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app only removes the records on that device; learning history stored on the server is deleted only when you delete your account. Items that applicable law requires us to keep are stored separately for the required period and then destroyed, and some records remain, without your name or email address, linked to the account number the Service assigned to you, to prevent abuse and to avoid giving you the same sentence again (everything that remains is listed in "4. Retention and destruction" below).

1. Data we collect and process

  • Account information: The email address and display name provided at social sign-in (such as Google), and an account identifier issued by the Service. (stored on server)
  • The answers you write: To grade your writing with AI, the sentences you write are sent to the grading providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) for grading and explanations. The grading process does not store your answers permanently on our server. Two exceptions — (1) if grading temporarily fails, the answer is kept in a retry queue for up to 14 days and then deleted; (2) if you submit a grading appeal, the task sentence, your answer, the score, and the explanation for that item are attached to your inquiry and retained. Once graded, your answers are saved to your account as part of your "Learning history" (below). (sent to third parties · stored on server as learning history)
  • Learning history: Review schedule (SRS) and progress (such as your level), the tasks you received with your answers, scores, and AI explanations, your “Ask the teacher” conversations (the questions you send and the AI's answers), favorites and notes, completed level test records (items, answers, scores, recommended level, and report), and some learning settings. Progress and weak-point statistics are calculated from these records. This data is stored on your device and, while you are signed in, also on the server for your account (synced), so you can continue on another device by signing in with the same account. Records made while you are offline are saved once you are connected. (stored on device · stored on server)
  • Level test: The grading process does not keep test items or your answers on the server, but the level test report (the AI evaluation text) is stored on the server so that the result is not lost (so you can see it again even if the app closes before you receive it). Completed level test records (items, answers, scores, and report) are saved to your account as part of your "Learning history" (above). They are deleted together with your account. (stored on server)
  • Generated content: Practice sentences generated by the Service (task sentences, not your answers) and per-account exposure history (to prevent repeated tasks and maintain content quality). (stored on server)
  • Subscription/payment status: Subscription tier (free/subscribed) and payment records (subscription product, price, currency, country of the store where you paid, cancellation reason). Actual payments and receipts are handled by the app market (Google Play) and the payment service provider (RevenueCat); the Service does not store payment-method details such as card numbers. (stored on server)
  • Usage records: Per-feature usage counts (quotas), the days you used the app, how many times you opened the payment screen, and error-diagnosis logs. (stored on server)
  • Values stored on your device: The sign-in session token (kept in your device's secure storage).
  • Device identifier: To prevent abuse (collecting free benefits more than once, repeated refunds), we collect the device marker Android assigns to this app. It is not a phone number or a hardware serial number (such as an IMEI), and a different value is given to other developers' apps, so it cannot be used to track you across apps. This value survives uninstalling and reinstalling the app, and changes when the device is factory reset. (stored on server)
    The advertising identifier (ad ID) used to serve ads is separate from this and is sent directly to Google by the ads SDK (see 8. Advertising below). (not stored on our server)
  • Device verification results: To detect requests that did not come through the app and other abuse, when you sign in we record the app and device verification result determined by Google Play (whether it is the genuine app, whether the device is genuine, and whether the app was obtained from Google Play), together with your account identifier and device marker. The verification request is processed by Google Play. (stored on server)
  • App update checks: Each time you open the app, it checks with the app update delivery service (Expo) whether a new version is available. Your device's operating system and app version information, and a number generated randomly for each installation, are sent at that time; no account information is included. (sent to third parties · not stored on our server)
  • Usage statistics (optional): Sent only if you turn on “Send usage statistics” in the app — nothing is sent before you turn it on (see 8-1 below).
    • What is sent: app usage records such as screen changes, time spent, and app opens, updates, and removal; subscription purchase records (product and price); ad records (when ads are shown to free users — ad requests, impressions and clicks, and how long an ad was on screen); device information (manufacturer, model, operating system version, device language, app version, and the store the app was installed from); approximate location estimated from your IP address (country, region, and city); and a random number newly generated for each installation of the app.
    • What is not sent: the sentences you write, account information (email, name, account identifier), the advertising identifier (ad ID), or the Android device ID.
    Processed by Google Analytics (Firebase). (sent to third parties · not stored on our server)
  • Inquiries and feedback: The title, body, and type of the feedback you send, plus context such as the screen, app version, and language pair at the time of submission. (stored on server · sent to operator alert channel)
  • Reports of harmful content (🚩): When you report text generated by AI, we receive the reason, the report target, the reported text itself, any description you write (optional), and the screen, app version, and language pair at the time of submission, so that we can investigate. (stored on server · sent to operator alert channel)
    • The reported text depends on the target — Given sentence · Feedback · Nuance explanation · Other ways to say it · Expressions to learn · Teacher's answer · Level test report.
    • If you report a "Teacher's answer", the whole conversation is submitted with it — including the questions you asked. We need to see which question produced that answer in order to fix the cause, and the conversation is delivered with the speakers marked.
    • Reporting from a practice item also sends that item's given sentence (whichever target you chose) — this identifies the item in question and lets us exclude that sentence from future tasks if necessary. Reports of a level test report are an exception and carry no given sentence, because the report is not text belonging to one specific item.
    • To prevent the same content being reported repeatedly, we also store an irreversibly transformed value (a fingerprint) of the reported text.

2. Purposes of use

  • Providing the learning service: AI grading of your writing with explanations and alternatives, and generation of writing tasks.
  • Maintaining your account and session (automatic sign-in) and providing features according to subscription status.
  • Managing free-tier limits and preventing misuse and abuse.
  • Improving service quality and responding to errors.
  • Delivering app updates.
  • Improving the app (only if usage statistics are turned on) — used to find the features you use most and the screens that are confusing, and to fix them.
  • Receiving and responding to user inquiries and feedback.
  • Receiving, investigating, and acting on reports of harmful content — used to exclude the offending given sentence from future tasks, or to correct the AI grading and task-generation rules.

4. Retention and destruction

  • Personal data stored on the server is retained until you close your account or request deletion, and is destroyed without delay once the purpose is fulfilled or upon request.
  • Sentences sent for grading to the AI providers (OpenAI and Anthropic — see 3. above) are processed for grading purposes, and the grading process does not store them permanently on our server (if grading temporarily fails, an answer is kept briefly in a retry queue and then deleted). Once graded, your answers are saved to your account as learning history (below).
  • Learning history is kept until you delete your account. When you delete your account, the learning history on the server and the records on the device where you deleted it are erased together (records remaining on other devices are removed by uninstalling the app there). When you delete a record in the app (such as an answer, a favorite, or a note), the copy stored on the server is deleted too; only a marker indicating what was deleted remains until you delete your account, so that the deletion also reaches your other devices. Uninstalling the app only removes the records on that device.
  • Records of harmful-content reports are retained for the action history (which sentence was excluded and why) and to detect repeat reports, and are deleted from our server together with your account. However, the copy delivered to the operator alert channel (Telegram) at the time of submission remains in that channel (§3) — it is an operational record; if you would like it deleted, please contact us at §10.
  • Usage statistics are deleted automatically every month once they are 14 months old in Google Analytics. Statistics aggregated so that no individual can be identified (for example, the number of users per day) remain after that. Because usage statistics are not linked to your account, deleting your account does not delete them separately; they are deleted according to this period.
  • Where applicable law requires retention, data is stored separately for the required period and then destroyed.
  • Minimal retention to prevent abuse: When you delete your account, your name, email address and sign-in account details are erased. The following remain — (1) the account number the Service assigned to you (a random number that links the records below, including when you signed up and deleted the account; if the same account or the same device returns, the records continue under this number), (2) an irreversibly hashed value of your sign-in account (used only to recognise that the same account has returned and whether it has already used the free level test), (3) a device marker (whether it is the same device), (4) a record of how much you used (dates and counts only), (5) a record of extra allowances granted (dates and counts only), (6) how many questions you used (a hashed item value and counts only — the content of your questions is not kept), (7) the list of sentences you have already been given (sentence identifiers and timestamps only), (8) your measured level (language pair and level only), (9) a record of promotion codes used (the code and time only), (10) refund and resubscription history (the device marker and event type only), (11) device verification results (the result Google Play gave at sign-in and when, including the device marker), and (12) records of lifting the one-free-account-per-device limit (requests to lift it and how they were handled — device markers, other account numbers used on the same device, and operator notes).
    None of these contain your name, email address, or the sentences you wrote, and they are not used for any other purpose. There are two purposes: to prevent repeatedly collecting free benefits or promotion codes, repeating refunds, or repeating sign-ups that did not come through the app by deleting and recreating an account, and to make sure the same sentence is not given to you again. These values alone cannot identify you.
    ⚠️ If you start over with a different account on the same device, the usage already recorded on that device carries over (this includes second-hand and shared devices).

5. Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your data, and may withdraw consent.
  • Account and data deletion can be requested in the app's settings or via the contact address below; upon request, the relevant data is destroyed.
  • Usage statistics can be turned off at any time in Settings › Account › “Send usage statistics”; once turned off, nothing more is sent.

6. Children's personal data

The Service is not primarily directed at children under 14. If we learn that a user is under 14, we destroy any data collected without legal-guardian consent.

7. Security measures

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), session tokens kept in device secure storage, no password storage (social sign-in), and minimized access privileges.

8. Advertising

  • We serve Google AdMob advertising to free users. No advertising is shown while you have a paid subscription.
  • When ads are served, the advertising identifier (ad ID) and app set ID, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), IP address, and app usage and diagnostic information (app launches and taps; app launch time, hangs and battery use) are sent to Google. If you turned on usage statistics, the per-installation random number used for usage statistics may also be included in ad requests. These values are not collected or stored by the app itself; the ads SDK includes them in the ad request, and they are not stored on our server.
  • You can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions, the app asks for your consent to advertising and data use when it starts, and personalized advertising is limited according to your choice. You can change your consent settings again in the app.

8-1. Usage statistics (automatic collection tool)

  • The app includes a Google Analytics (Firebase) feature that collects usage statistics. This feature is turned off by default and runs only if you choose “Turn on” on the in-app card (shown after you finish your first writing exercise) or in Settings. Nothing is sent before you turn it on.
  • If you turn it on, what is and is not sent is the same as “Usage statistics (optional)” in 1. above. So that the data is not used for advertising, all advertising-related consents (ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization) are sent as “denied”. However, when ads are shown to free users, the ads SDK may include the per-installation random number used for usage statistics in ad requests (see 8. above).
  • Refusal and withdrawal: Turn off Settings › Account › “Send usage statistics” and nothing more is sent from then on. You can use every feature whether or not you turn it on.
  • This choice is stored on the device: signing out or deleting your account does not change it, and it also applies to other accounts on the same device (uninstalling and reinstalling the app, or clearing the app's data in Android settings, turns it back off). To change it, use Settings › Account › “Send usage statistics”.
