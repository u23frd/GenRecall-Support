Privacy Policy

GenRecall

Effective date: 2026-06-22 · Last revised: 2026-09-05

This English translation is provided for convenience only. The Korean original is the legally binding version and prevails in case of any discrepancy.

GenRecall (the "Service") takes your personal data seriously and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what data the Service collects and how it is used, stored, and destroyed.

Summary: Your learning history (the answers you write, scores, review schedule, statistics, favorites, and notes) is stored on your device and is not synced to the server. The server stores information needed to run the Service, such as your account, subscription, and usage. To grade your writing with AI, the sentences you write are sent to the grading providers (OpenAI, Anthropic). Free users are shown advertising, and an advertising identifier is sent to Google when ads are served.

Account and data deletion requests

If you would like your GenRecall account and related data deleted, you can request it in either of these ways.

  • In the app: Use "Delete account" in the Settings screen to delete your server account and associated data, together with the learning history stored on your device.
  • By email: Write to support@existential-labs.com with "Account deletion request" in the subject line, including the email address you signed up with.

Within 7 business days of receiving a request, we delete the following data stored on the server: account information (email, name, account identifier), subscription status, level-test records and reviews, pending grading items, and feedback you sent. Learning records stored on your device are removed via "Delete account", "Reset everything", or by uninstalling the app. Items that applicable law requires us to keep are stored separately for the required period and then destroyed, and some records remain in a form that cannot identify you, to prevent abuse and to avoid giving you the same sentence again (everything that remains is listed in "4. Retention and destruction" below).

1. Data we collect and process

  • Account information: The email address and display name provided at social sign-in (such as Google), and an account identifier issued by the Service. (stored on server)
  • The answers you write: To grade your writing with AI, the sentences you write are sent to the grading providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) for grading and explanations. Answers are not stored permanently on our server. Two exceptions — (1) if grading temporarily fails, the answer is kept in a retry queue for up to 14 days and then deleted; (2) if you submit a grading appeal, the task sentence, your answer, the score, and the explanation for that item are attached to your inquiry and retained. (sent to third parties · not stored permanently on server)
  • Learning history: Review schedule (SRS), your answers and score history, progress and weak-point statistics, notes and favorites. This data is stored on your device and is not sent to or synced with the server. (stored on device)
  • Level test: Test items and your answers are not kept on the server after grading, but the level test report (the AI evaluation text) is stored on the server so that the result is not lost (so you can see it again even if the app closes before you receive it). It is deleted together with your account. (stored on server)
  • Generated content: Practice sentences generated by the Service (task sentences, not your answers) and per-account exposure history (to prevent repeated tasks and maintain content quality). (stored on server)
  • Subscription/payment status: Subscription tier (free/subscribed). Actual payments and receipts are handled by the app market (Google Play) and the payment service provider (RevenueCat); the Service does not store payment-method details such as card numbers. (stored on server)
  • Usage records: Per-feature usage counts (quotas) and error-diagnosis logs. (stored on server)
  • Values stored on your device: The sign-in session token (kept in your device's secure storage).
  • Device identifier: To prevent abuse (collecting free benefits more than once, repeated refunds), we collect the device marker Android assigns to this app. It is not a phone number or a hardware serial number (such as an IMEI), and a different value is given to other developers' apps, so it cannot be used to track you across apps. This value survives uninstalling and reinstalling the app, and changes when the device is factory reset. (stored on server)
    The advertising identifier (ad ID) used to serve ads is separate from this and is sent directly to Google by the ads SDK (see 8. Advertising below). (not stored on our server)
  • Inquiries and feedback: The title, body, and type of the feedback you send, plus context such as the screen, app version, and language pair at the time of submission. (stored on server · sent to operator alert channel)
  • Reports of harmful content (🚩): When you report text generated by AI, we receive the reason, the report target, the reported text itself, any description you write (optional), and the screen, app version, and language pair at the time of submission, so that we can investigate. (stored on server · sent to operator alert channel)
    • The reported text depends on the target — Given sentence · Feedback · Nuance explanation · Other ways to say it · Expressions to learn · Teacher's answer · Level test report.
    • If you report a "Teacher's answer", the whole conversation is submitted with it — including the questions you asked. We need to see which question produced that answer in order to fix the cause, and the conversation is delivered with the speakers marked.
    • Reporting from a practice item also sends that item's given sentence (whichever target you chose) — this identifies the item in question and lets us exclude that sentence from future tasks if necessary. Reports of a level test report are an exception and carry no given sentence, because the report is not text belonging to one specific item.
    • To prevent the same content being reported repeatedly, we also store an irreversibly transformed value (a fingerprint) of the reported text.

2. Purposes of use

  • Providing the learning service: AI grading of your writing with explanations and alternatives, and generation of writing tasks.
  • Maintaining your account and session (automatic sign-in) and providing features according to subscription status.
  • Managing free-tier limits and preventing misuse and abuse.
  • Improving service quality and responding to errors.
  • Receiving and responding to user inquiries and feedback.
  • Receiving, investigating, and acting on reports of harmful content — used to exclude the offending given sentence from future tasks, or to correct the AI grading and task-generation rules.

4. Retention and destruction

  • Personal data stored on the server is retained until you close your account or request deletion, and is destroyed without delay once the purpose is fulfilled or upon request.
  • Sentences sent to Anthropic (or to OpenAI if you have consented to backup grading) are processed for grading purposes and are not stored permanently on our server (if grading temporarily fails, an answer is kept briefly in a retry queue and then deleted).
  • Learning history stored on your device can be removed at any time using the app's delete/reset features or by uninstalling the app.
  • Records of harmful-content reports are retained for the action history (which sentence was excluded and why) and to detect repeat reports, and are deleted from our server together with your account. However, the copy delivered to the operator alert channel (Telegram) at the time of submission remains in that channel (§3) — it is an operational record; if you would like it deleted, please contact us at §10.
  • Where applicable law requires retention, data is stored separately for the required period and then destroyed.
  • Minimal retention to prevent abuse: When you delete your account, your name, email address and account identifier are erased. The following remain — (1) an irreversibly hashed value of your sign-in account (used only to recognise that the same account has returned), (2) a device marker (whether it is the same device), (3) a record of how much you used (dates and counts only), (4) a record of extra allowances granted (dates and counts only), (5) how many questions you used (a hashed item value and counts only — the content of your questions is not stored), (6) the list of sentences you have already been given (sentence identifiers and timestamps only), and (7) refund and resubscription history (the device marker and event type only).
    None of these contain your name, email address, or the sentences you wrote, and they are not used for any other purpose. There are two purposes: to prevent repeatedly collecting free benefits or repeating refunds by deleting and recreating an account, and to make sure the same sentence is not given to you again. These values alone cannot identify you.
    ⚠️ If you start over with a different account on the same device, the usage already recorded on that device carries over (this includes second-hand and shared devices).

5. Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your data, and may withdraw consent.
  • Account and data deletion can be requested in the app's settings or via the contact address below; upon request, the relevant data is destroyed.

6. Children's personal data

The Service is not primarily directed at children under 14. If we learn that a user is under 14, we destroy any data collected without legal-guardian consent.

7. Security measures

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), session tokens kept in device secure storage, no password storage (social sign-in), and minimized access privileges.

8. Advertising

  • We serve Google AdMob advertising to free users. No advertising is shown while you have a paid subscription.
  • When ads are served, the advertising identifier (ad ID), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and IP address are sent to Google. These values are not collected or stored by the app itself; the ads SDK includes them in the ad request, and they are not stored on our server.
  • You can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions, the app asks for your consent to advertising and data use when it starts, and personalized advertising is limited according to your choice. You can change your consent settings again in the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, notice will be given in the app or on a posted page.
