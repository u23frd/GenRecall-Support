Understanding a language and being able to write it are never the same thing.

Existential Labs builds language-learning tools that get learners writing real sentences in a foreign language. There is already plenty of reading and listening practice out there. What is missing is practice where you build a sentence yourself — and get told, right away, why it sounds off.

Product

GenRecall

See the meaning in your language, then write the sentence in the language you are learning. AI grades it and explains what to fix — and why.

  • Learn by writing

    No picking from options — you write the sentence yourself. Recalling and producing is what makes it stick.

  • Grading that explains why it sounds off

    Not just right or wrong. You see the natural way to say it, together with the reason.

  • Difficulty that adapts to you

    Start with a level test; difficulty adjusts automatically as you practice.
